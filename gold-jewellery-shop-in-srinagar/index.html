Pyramid Golds: a gold shop at Habak Crossing

Pyramid Golds is a gold and jewellery shop in Srinagar, opposite Dr. Dobhi at Habak Crossing. We sell hallmarked gold and silver jewellery and MMTC-PAMP certified gold coins, bars and biscuits, and we buy back certified bullion after checking it in the store.

MMTC-PAMP gold coins in Srinagar

Our bullion is MMTC-PAMP: 999.9 fine (24 karat) gold, each piece sealed in a tamper-proof assay card that shows its weight, purity and serial number. Keep the card sealed and the piece is easy to verify and easy to sell back later. See every weight we stock on our MMTC-PAMP gold coins and bars page.
