Understanding hallmarked gold

What the BIS hallmark and HUID on your gold jewellery mean, and how to check them yourself in under a minute.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

What is a hallmark?

A hallmark is an official mark that certifies the purity of a precious metal article. In India, gold hallmarking is run by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS). Jewellery is tested at a BIS-recognised Assaying and Hallmarking Centre, and the marks are laser-engraved onto the piece.

At Pyramid Golds every gold and silver piece we sell carries official hallmark certification, so you know exactly what you are buying.

The three marks on hallmarked gold

Current BIS hallmarks on gold jewellery have three parts:

  1. The BIS standard mark, a small triangle logo.
  2. The purity grade, written as karat and fineness, for example 22K916.
  3. The HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification), a 6-character alphanumeric code unique to that piece.

Older jewellery may carry a different set of marks, such as the jeweller's mark and the year of marking. Those pieces were hallmarked under the earlier scheme.

How to check a hallmark in a minute

  1. Find the marks on the piece. They are tiny, so use your phone camera zoom or ask us for a loupe.
  2. Note the 6-character HUID.
  3. Open the official BIS CARE app and choose Verify HUID.
  4. Check that the purity and article type shown in the app match the piece and your invoice.

Hallmarking and silver

Silver can be hallmarked too, with its own fineness grades, such as 925 for sterling silver. Our silver jewellery is hallmarked; ask us to show you the marks on any piece.

What a hallmark does not tell you

A hallmark certifies purity. It does not tell you whether the price is fair. For that, look at the gold rate, the net weight, the making charges and the GST, all of which should be shown on your invoice. Our gold jewellery buying guide explains each line.

Frequently asked questions

Is hallmarking compulsory for gold jewellery in India?

Yes. BIS hallmarking with a 6-character HUID is mandatory for gold jewellery and artefacts sold by jewellers in the districts notified under the rules, a list BIS has widened in phases since 2021. Ask the jeweller if you are unsure.

Can I check a hallmark on my phone?

Yes. Install the official BIS CARE app, choose Verify HUID and type the 6-character code from the piece. The app shows the purity, the type of article and the hallmarking centre.

Does a hallmark mean the price is fair?

No. A hallmark certifies purity, not price. Compare the gold rate, the weight, the making charges and the GST on your invoice.
