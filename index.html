Our Story

Jewellery rooted
in Kashmir.

Pyramid Golds was built on one simple belief — that quality jewellery should be accessible, verifiable, and beautiful. Every piece we carry is hallmarked and authentic, because we think you deserve to know exactly what you are wearing.

We are based in Srinagar, Kashmir — a place with a long and proud tradition of fine craftsmanship. That heritage runs through everything we do, from the pieces we select to the way we present them.

Whether you are buying for yourself or for someone you love, we take that seriously. This is not just jewellery. It is something you will keep.
