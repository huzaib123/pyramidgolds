Coins from 1 g to 50 g are the most popular way to start buying gold and a meaningful gift for a wedding, nikah, Eid or Diwali. Small weights let you build up a holding a little at a time.

Gold bars and biscuits

Bars run from 10 g to 1 kg. Larger bars carry a lower premium per gram than coins, which makes them a better fit for long-term savings. The 100 g biscuit and the traditional 10 tola biscuit (116.64 g) are the classic forms for holding gold in Kashmir: simple to store, easy to verify and quick to resell.

About MMTC-PAMP

MMTC-PAMP India is a joint venture between MMTC Ltd, a Government of India enterprise, and PAMP SA of Switzerland. It refines and mints gold at its facility in Haryana and sells it as sealed, serial-numbered bars and coins. For buyers, the practical benefits are:

  • Known purity. 999.9 fine gold, stated on every assay card.
  • Traceability. Each piece has its own serial number.
  • Easy resale. A sealed, recognised product is simple for any buyer to check.

Keep the assay card sealed and store it somewhere dry. Opening the card does not change the gold, but it makes the piece harder to verify when you come to sell. Read more in our guide to buying gold coins and bars.

Frequently asked questions

Are your MMTC-PAMP coins and bars genuine?

Yes. Every coin, bar and biscuit comes sealed in its original MMTC-PAMP assay card, which shows the weight, the 999.9 purity and a unique serial number.

What does 999.9 mean?

It means the gold is 99.99% pure, often called four nines fine gold. In karat terms it is 24 karat.

How much is a 10 tola gold biscuit?

A tola is about 11.66 g, so a 10 tola biscuit weighs 116.64 g. The price follows the live gold rate, so message us on WhatsApp for an exact quote.

Do you buy back MMTC-PAMP gold?

Yes. We buy back certified bullion after a physical evaluation at our Srinagar store. Bring the piece in its assay card and we quote a fair rate on the spot.

Do I need ID to buy gold bullion?

Large bullion purchases may need standard KYC, such as your ID or PAN, at the time of invoicing. Every purchase comes with a full GST invoice.
